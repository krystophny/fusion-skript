# Energy inputs and visual provenance

Numeric inputs in `energy.json` were checked on 2026-10-05 against Statistics
Austria's [preliminary 2025 energy balance](https://www.statistik.at/fileadmin/pages/99/preliminaryEnergyBalancesforAustriaInTerajoule.ods)
(Balance sheet) and [annual average population](https://www.statistik.at/en/statistics/population-and-society/population/population-stock/annual-average-population).
The balance uses total column AL: rows 8/13 for primary/final energy; rows
14/15/17/18/19 for industry, transport, services, households and agriculture.
Energy data are preliminary; population was compiled on 2026-06-29.
Provider [terms](https://www.statistik.at/en/about-us/responsibilities-and-principles/legal-basis/general-terms-and-conditions)
and [open-data terms](https://data.statistik.gv.at/web/?page=terms) remain
separate from the licence of our independently designed figures.

## Reuse rule for this chapter

For data series and numerical facts, the lecturer accepts sources that permit
free reuse with attribution. Statistics Austria, PVGIS, IRENA and BGR values
are credited under their own stated or custom terms and are not labelled CC
unless the source says so. Captions name providers and relevant terms;
`sources.json` records the source URL, access date, year, transformation and
status. Third-party images and figures must be public domain, CC0, CC BY or
CC BY-SA. No provider chart artwork is reproduced. NonCommercial sources remain
excluded from public outputs.
The NREL source terms are linked in the registry; an HTML page snapshot is
retained locally as ignored research evidence. Its complete required notice
accompanies the derived CSV, plot-data JSON and SVG at
`data/ch00/processed/nrel-lifecycle-notice.txt`; plots credit DOE/NREL/ALLIANCE.

No provider illustration, chart or table image is included. Resource and area
plots are explicitly stated model scenarios, not empirical resource inventories
or site-specific renewable yields. MacKay's *Sustainable Energy—Without the
Hot Air* (2009), https://www.withouthotair.com/, motivates the quantitative
comparisons; its prose and artwork have not been incorporated.

## Plot contract

Original plots compare energy accounting, country energy and GDP, solar resource,
prices, land power density, bird mortality, energy deaths and lifecycle emissions.
Axes carry units; plot-data files carry a source caption. Chapter derivations are
in `derivations/chapters/ch00_energy_context.py`; simple light-theme SVGs are
generated by `scripts/plot-ch00-teaching.py`. Existing Austria budget figures
remain in `derivations/energy.py`. Publication styling can change without
changing these data or source captions.

## Chapter 0 source and calculation handoff (2026-10-06)

This addition prepares empirical inputs, numerical derivations and rough checks;
it does not change the deck, theme or established plot styling. The complete
numerical ledger is [NUMBERS.md](../NUMBERS.md). Every substituted constant has
a value, unit, year and source key in [inputs.json](ch00/processed/inputs.json).
The machine-readable source registry is [sources.json](ch00/sources.json).
The detailed recipe and gaps are in [the handoff](ch00/README.md).

Source snapshots in `ch00/raw/` are **research evidence, not a public asset
directory**. They include copyrighted and NC candidates that must not be copied
to public outputs. [raw-manifest.json](ch00/raw-manifest.json) records hashes
only for non-ignored source snapshots; private or restricted local candidates
are deliberately omitted. Source and provider licences are not replaced by our
CC BY licence.
In particular, an OWID/WDI blanket CC notice does not override specific
underlying terms, and data.gv.at's usual licence does not automatically cover a
Statistics Austria file hosted elsewhere.

system Python here). Use `uv run --with openpyxl --with pillow python ...` if
needed. The main derivation and check-plot scripts use the existing project
dependencies. See the geographic handoff for its isolated optional dependencies.

## Electricity-cost sourcebook, 9 October 2026

The supplied text and four complete CSV tables are preserved verbatim in
[`ch00/raw/cost-sourcebook/`](ch00/raw/cost-sourcebook/); its manifest records
checksums, units and the original evidence flags. The
[`electricity-cost-audit.json`](ch00/processed/electricity-cost-audit.json)
records primary-source checks and the downstream correction of the 2010
installed-battery point from a benchmark flag to a published retrospective
model. The raw table remains unchanged. Historical values use the supplied
extract and approximate 2025-dollar rebasing, not a freshly assembled series.

IRENA global renewable benchmarks, Lazard US new-build estimates and author
storage scenarios have different boundaries. In particular, the daily model
is not a firm-supply cost, and the Nevada model's 95% denominator is annual
energy demand. The oil fuel proxy is retained in the archive but not plotted
as LCOE. Module prices, battery capacity prices and delivered-electricity costs
have separate axes and units.

`derivations/chapters/ch00_electricity_costs.py` emits a typed, audited plotting
payload and independently recomputes all 48 supplied daily-storage results;
the maximum difference must stay below 0.001 US cents/kWh (rounding tolerance).
`scripts/plot-electricity-costs.py` draws the two canonical figures reused in
the reference and slides. Both steps run in `scripts/build-site.sh`.
Original figures and derivations follow this project's licences. Numerical
facts retain the source registry's provider attribution; the ingested source
material does not acquire a blanket CC licence. No provider chart is reused.

## Living-standards comparison, 12 October 2026

The living-standards comparison retains its raw PIP responses at each cutoff,
the EIA 2000 primary-energy response (alongside the existing 2024 response),
and the WDI Austria/Ethiopia population response for 1981–2024. Its electricity
access figures use the existing WDI snapshot: 78.224965% in 2000 and 91.926484%
in 2024. The Austria/Ethiopia ratio is a comparison within EIA's primary-energy
boundary; traditional biomass is not fully covered. The derived counts,
percentages and energy ratios live in `03-income-levels.json`, generated by
`derivations/chapters/ch00_energy_context.py`. The canonical 1981/2000/2024 SVGs use
identical axes and are reused unchanged in the slide deck and reference chapter.

1981 is the first global year in PIP. WDI has no global electricity-access
observation for 1981; its slide shows that absence explicitly, without imputation.

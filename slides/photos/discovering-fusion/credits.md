# Discovering fusion: image provenance

Original FusionIntroSlides photographs, scientific figures and Christopher
Albert’s drawings have been restored for noncommercial university teaching.
Their source register and documented licences are in
[original/credits.md](original/credits.md). Third-party images retain their own
terms; the course’s original-content licence does not relicense those images.

on the hero page. False-colour ultraviolet image, not a visible-light view.

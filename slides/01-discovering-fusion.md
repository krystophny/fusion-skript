## Necessary changes

- Keep the new Sun photograph, as requested; restore original figures and
  photographs instead of the earlier substitute diagrams and photographs.
- Retain separate Aston plates and the three successive reaction cartoons.
  Typeset labels and equations natively over the original artwork.
- Keep every page dark. Retain Eddington’s full English quotation and original
  emphasis on page 7. Page 9 retains the full 1929 abstract in faithful English
  translation and its original emphases, together with the translated title.
- Preserve the Lawson diagram on page 15; space its symbols, original arrows,
  coffee cup and portrait clearly. Do not substitute new explanatory content.
- Express the original density and pressure examples in SI. Use rho for mass
  density rather than n. These are approximate illustrative parameters.
- On page 16 restore the Sun’s pressure–confinement product and the original
  p–p comparison. The roughly ten-million-year time is a thermal timescale
  (stored thermal energy / luminosity), not the Sun’s age. The earlier removal
  misidentified this timescale. Source: Princeton solar-interior lecture notes,
  https://www.astro.princeton.edu/~burrows/classes/204/sun.interior.pdf .
- The Lawson pressure-time scale is an approximate D-T guide near optimal
  temperature, not a universal threshold for every fuel or operating point.
  Keep its historical 1955 date; cite the 1957 publication.
- Keep the already agreed introductory plant and tritium-loop slides. Detailed
  engineering stays in Chapter 10; reaction-rate and Lawson derivations in
  Chapters 3–4; pressure gradients and force balance in Chapter 6.
- Original source slides 4–5 repeat Chapter 0; the Japanese Earth/Sun bridge
  and later SPARC, ITER, equilibrium, transport and research material remain
  outside this chapter’s selected discovery sequence.
